# Multi-Agent Research Assistant: Roles and Challenges
## Executive Summary
This research report explores the roles of different agents in a multi-agent research assistant system and the challenges associated with designing and implementing such systems. The findings suggest that the research agent is responsible for finding information, the analyzer for picking out key points, the critic for checking for mistakes, and the writer for preparing the final report. The main challenges identified are the increased time and model calls required, and the potential for errors to propagate through the system. However, the current findings do not cover how agents communicate within the system or the decision-making processes of individual agents.

## Key Findings

### Roles of Different Agents in a Multi-Agent System
[1] In a research assistant, the roles of the agents are as follows:
- The **research agent** is responsible for finding relevant information.
- The **analyzer** selects and highlights the most important points from the gathered information.
- The **critic** reviews the work to ensure accuracy and correctness.
- The **writer** compiles the analyzed information into a final report.

### Challenges in Designing and Implementing Multi-Agent Systems
[1] The main challenges in designing and implementing a multi-agent system include:
- It takes more time to develop and manage the system.
- There are more model calls required for each agent to operate.
- A mistake made by one agent can be passed on to the next agent, leading to potential errors in the final output.
