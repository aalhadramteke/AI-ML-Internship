The economic instability, including inflation and taxation, led to social unrest and economic hardship among the populace. ### Military Factors
[1] Military factors such as the inability to maintain a large, professional army and the constant threat from barbarian invasions were significant contributors to the fall of the Roman Empire. The Roman military, while formidable in the past, struggled to maintain a large, professional army due to various factors, including financial constraints and the empire's geographical spread. The constant threat from barbarian invasions, particularly from the Huns and later the Visigoths, put immense pressure on the Roman military and contributed to the empire's eventual collapse.
